# WedEazzy — The Smart Platform for Banquet Halls

> **Showcase Your Venue. Get Booked. Grow Your Business.**

WedEazzy is India’s dedicated smart platform for luxury banquet halls, hotel ballrooms, and wedding lawns. It connects banquet owners and general managers directly with thousands of verified couples, wedding planners, and corporate event organizers.

---

## ✨ Features Implemented

1. **Royal Architectural Hero Section**
   - Asymmetric grand architectural arch framing opulent ballroom chandelier photography.
   - Distinctive typography: **Playfair Display & Cinzel** paired with **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
   - Triple core value pillars: **Grow**, **Connect**, and **Succeed**.
   - Signature floating burgundy badge: *"More Visibility. More Bookings. More Business."*

2. **Why Banquet Halls Choose WedEazzy (6 Bento Cards)**
   - **Increase Visibility** (120K+ monthly searches)
   - **Verified & Trusted** (100% verified profile)
   - **Bookings That Grow** (3.4x average inquiry growth, 0% commission)
   - **Showcase Your Venue** (4K photo & 360° virtual tours)
   - **Manage Easily** (Lead CRM & calendar sync)
   - **Dedicated Support** (24/7 Relationship Manager)

3. **Reach the Right Audience & Cross-Device Showcase**
   - Interactive buyer segment selector: *Engaged Couples*, *Wedding Planners*, *Event Managers*, *Corporate Clients*, and *Social Gatherings*.
   - Live multi-device interactive frame previewing both the **Desktop Marketplace** and the **Mobile Couple View**.

4. **Interactive Banquet ROI & Revenue Calculator (Creative Module)**
   - Real-time sliders for **Guest Capacity (200 - 3,000)**, **Average Plate Price (₹1,200 - ₹5,000)**, and **Unbooked Target Dates**.
   - Instant calculation of inquiries, confirmed bookings, and projected incremental revenue formatted in Indian Rupee notation (`₹ Lakhs`).
   - One-click trigger pre-filling the onboarding wizard with calculated capacity.

5. **Dual Perspective Switcher (Creative Module)**
   - Seamlessly toggle between:
     - **🏢 Owner Dashboard & CRM:** Inbound WhatsApp leads, occupancy tracking, and revenue overview.
     - **👰 Couple Discovery Experience:** 360° virtual walk-through, pricing tiers, and verified guest reviews.

6. **Everything You Need to Succeed (Feature Suite)**
   - 6 diamond-accented operational badges: *Premium Listing*, *Photo & Virtual Tour Gallery*, *Service Highlights*, *Real Reviews*, *Enquiry Management*, and *Performance Insights*.

7. **5-Card Luxury Ballroom Showcase with Lightbox**
   - 5 curated luxury ballroom setups matching the reference flyer (chandeliers, floral mandap, cocktail lounge, gala round tables, royal bridal aisle).
   - Interactive Lightbox modal showing capacity, city, plate price, amenities, and listing triggers.

8. **Real Banquet Hall Owner Success Stories**
   - Verified testimonials with concrete booking and revenue statistics from Delhi NCR, Pune, and Jaipur.

9. **Pre-Footer CTA Banner & Deep Royal Wine Footer**
   - Prominent helpline dialer: `+91 7498987620` (click-to-call).
   - Official domain link: `WedEazzy.com`.
   - Corner botanical floral filigree flourishes.

10. **Interactive 3-Step Venue Onboarding Wizard**
    - Step 1: Venue Name, City, and Category.
    - Step 2: Capacity, Plate Price, and Amenities Multi-Select.
    - Step 3: Owner Name, WhatsApp Number (+91 validation), and Simulated Relationship Manager assignment with celebratory confetti.

11. **Floating Quick Help & WhatsApp Bubble**
    - Instant click-to-WhatsApp with pre-filled greeting and phone dialer.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Install Dependencies
```bash
pnpm install
```

### 2. Run Local Development Server
```bash
pnpm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

### 3. Build for Production
```bash
pnpm run build
```
